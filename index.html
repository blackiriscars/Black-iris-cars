Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do I need to rent a car?

You need a valid driver's license (held for at least 2 years), a passport or ID card.

Is insurance included in the price?

Yes, We offer comprehensive coverage (All Risks) options for complete peace of mind.

Do you require a security deposit?

Yes, a security deposit is required. The amount depends on the car category (usually between 5,000 DH and 20,000 DH). It is fully refundable upon safe return of the vehicle.

Can you deliver the car to my hotel?

Absolutely. We offer free delivery to airports and hotels within the city centers of Rabat, Temara and Casablanca.
